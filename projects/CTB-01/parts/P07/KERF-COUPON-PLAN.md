# Future physical validation plan — KERF_COUPON_PENDING

Planning only; no actual material cutting/testing performed or authorized by CTB007.
A qualified lead must approve a separate physical trial before these hypotheses are used.
The 4mm nominal birch/50mm height is the subject to validate, not a successful recipe.

Record actual stock thickness/ply orientation/defects/moisture, machine/kerf and both facegrain
orientations; identify coupons and source revision. Measure the relation between cut slot
width, remaining web, neutral-axis length, springback and radius at full 50mm height. The
source0.8mm openings and8mm adjacent pitch are geometric study values, not measured laser
kerf or a recommended production setting. Compare alternatives and reject torn/unstable webs.

Test seam closure tolerances/contact in the intended insertion direction: A butt gaps,
B horizontal steps (zero nominal vertical clearance), C edge notches/tabs. Measure form after
fixture removal and adhesive cure, edge registration and resistance to opening/pullout. Glue
selection, joint preparation, clamp/holding sequence, cure, creep, humidity and material
bearing all need defined acceptance criteria and assessment; none can be inferred from CAD.

Evaluate filler/sanding as finish only, including shrinkage, cracking and smooth external
edge. It is not structural reinforcement. Establish load/deflection/stability cases separately
with competent review; this study supplies no capacity or furniture-use authorization.

Keep geometric strip development R173 separate from empirical neutral axis. If measurements
change length/pitch/notches, version source, regenerate all pieces, check cumulative seams,
skin/rim/rib interfaces, exact assembly and independent QA before any later G3/G4 decision.
