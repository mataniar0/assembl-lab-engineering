## 2. Analytical envelope checks (no CAD motion proof)
Assuming purely for a **maximum initial flat rectangular clearance bound** that four planar walls each occupy 6mm at their ends (no frame overlap or intrusions), the available planar interior is `(260-2×6)×(160-2×6)=248×148mm`. The reference etrog bounding footprint `200×100mm` leaves **48mm total nominal remainder** in each horizontal direction (24mm/side *if centered*). This is an optimistic geometric bound before any corner layers, H1 support, tray, lining, fruit irregularity and tolerance. It is **not** established usable cavity or fit.
For a **surrogate straight lid ray** of reach `R=160mm`, hinged at its rear end with closed ray toward -Y: `ΔY(θ)=-R cos θ`, `ΔZ(θ)=R sin θ`. At 110° the end projects **54.72mm behind hinge axis**, and at 120° **80mm**: +25.28mm more rear projection. These are comparison numbers only, **not** swept-volume clearance of the real ETG arched lid, and do not imply any actual pivot coordinate, opening geometry or collision result.

## 3. Exactly ONE architectural candidate — H1-ETG-C1, layered recessed fork
- Use **rear P06 laminate stacks as fixed supports**, partly composed of two *stationary* thin plywood leaves separated by one *moving* P09 (left) or P10 (right) lid cheek, forming a concealed three-plate fork. Exterior ring/disk should not protrude. Both hinge groups have one shared world-X pivot axis; local left/right assembly is mirror-imaged.
- Two real **P11** wooden pins (one per hinge), each passes the fixed/moving/fixed sandwich. **P12** low-profile caps mechanically trap the pin on the exterior side, by a proposed stepped/captive recess formed from stacked P06 cut profiles — **not** a fictional laser-engraved precision depth or friction-only push cap. Need physically plausible flat profiles, closure order, replacement access and negative cap-absent extraction tests.
- `t_fixedOuter + g1 + t_moving + g2 + t_fixedInner` is pin bearing-travel minimum. With three 6mm nominal leaves it is **18mm + g1 + g2**, not counting shoulder, cap stack, head or real insertion space. Therefore **the 12mm-long two×6mm MEB study pin would be at least 6mm short even if both axial gaps were zero**; copying it is rejected **for this three-plate candidate only**. Define a new ETG pin design and retainer path in B1; do not import MEB nominal gaps as measured ETG tolerances.
- Layered P06 supports might require altering original 96 slices. All affected P02/P03/P04 body joins, P07/P08 lid supports and P13 tray must be checked; historic P06 geometry and count are reference only, not proven H1 source. The candidate is a **mechanical hypothesis**, not CAD PASS.
